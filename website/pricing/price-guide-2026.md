# Log Home Finishing LLC: 2026 price guide (approved for the website by Tom Elliott, Nov 18 2026)

Built from line items on Log Home Finishing estimates written between March and November 2026. Square-foot prices are per square foot of log wall surface. Linear-foot prices are per foot of joint or check. Every job is priced after an on-site inspection; ranges move with condition, access, elevation and travel.

| Paint encapsulation for homes with old paint or lead-bearing coatings | sq ft | from about $3 | When blasting isn't safe or legal; lab testing first |
